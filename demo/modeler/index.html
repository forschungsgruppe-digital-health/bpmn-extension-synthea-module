    Bring your own pathway

    Open your BPMN files (.bpmn or .xml) to edit them with the Synthea settings and see the Synthea module they become. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

    Drop .bpmn or .xml files, or a folder, here or choose
    1. Open one file or a whole folder, or try the real lung cancer pathway from Examples. A main pathway that calls submodules needs the submodule files open too, so open the folder.
    2. Edit: click an element and set its Synthea properties in the panel on the right.
    3. Convert to see the Synthea module, with messages that explain what to fix.

    New to this? Follow the Quickstart, which uses the examples. Reading Basics first helps.
